Given two real random variables $X$ and $Y$

Then the covariance is defined as

$$
Cov(X,Y) := \mathbb E[(X - \mathbb E[X]) \cdot (Y- \mathbb E[Y])]
$$

A positive covariance means that there is a common trend in the same direction in both variables.
So if $X$ has a positive trend then $Y$ has a positive trend too.

On the other hand a negative covariance means that there is trend in opposing direction ($X$ is positive $=>$ $Y$ will be negative).
